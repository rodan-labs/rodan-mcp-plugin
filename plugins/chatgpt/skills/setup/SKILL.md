# Set up Rodan

Tell the user, in three short sentences:

1. Rodan connects this assistant to their Rodan account. They sign in with their Rodan
   account, and Rodan shows only the tools for the apps they have.
2. Today the app is OmniaGraph. It runs monitors: repeated research on the markets,
   companies and regulators they follow. Each finding has a source and an OmniaScore.
3. Answering from stored findings is free. Starting research, creating a monitor, adding to a
   watchlist and uploading a document change their workspace, so you will ask first.

Then ask what they want to find out, for example what changed in a monitor this week.

If a tool says the connection has expired, give them
https://platform.rodananalytics.com/connections.

Do not ask for passwords, codes or tokens in the chat. Sign-in happens on the Rodan page.
